About Me

I am an Associate Researcher (特聘副研究员) at the School of Remote Sensing and Information Engineering, Wuhan University. My research focuses on GeoSpatial Artificial Intelligence (GeoAI), with a particular emphasis on leveraging deep learning techniques for remote sensing image understanding, change detection, and geospatial data mining.
